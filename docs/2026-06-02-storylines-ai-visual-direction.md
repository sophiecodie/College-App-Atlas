# Storylines AI Visual Direction

## Goal

Make the Storylines page feel more alive, visual, and idea-driven.

Instead of only showing static cards, the AI area should help the student feel like ideas are being surfaced, connected, and shaped into possible essay directions.

## Visual Direction

- Use animated little bubbles.
- Each bubble should contain a small idea, trait, fact, brainstorm fragment, or thematic phrase.
- The bubbles should feel light, dynamic, and exploratory rather than rigid or corporate.

## Interaction Idea

- Bubbles should move subtly so the page feels active.
- Lines should draw between related bubbles.
- Connections should appear as the system identifies patterns across different facts or themes.
- The visual should suggest that diverse and unique storylines are being formed in real time.

## Desired Effect

- Show that the AI is not just listing ideas.
- Show that it is discovering relationships between different parts of the student’s life.
- Make the process feel creative, surprising, and personal.

## Storyline Formation

- A single storyline can be represented by multiple bubbles connected together.
- Different clusters of bubbles can represent different possible essay angles.
- Some bubbles can connect across clusters to show overlap between themes.
- The final result should communicate that strong essay ideas come from combining unexpected details, not from generic summary.

## Product Notes

- Keep the experience student-friendly and easy to understand.
- Avoid making it feel like a generic AI dashboard or a data visualization tool.
- The animation should support comprehension, not distract from it.
- The visual system should make brainstorming feel playful, but still polished and intentional.
